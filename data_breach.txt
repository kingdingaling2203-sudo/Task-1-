The problem with email addresses and passwords being exposed publicly is that it can create a wide range of problems for that user, with private information being leaked or even sold to other people. It can also give access to attackers who have created false identities/profiles posing as reputable companies and banks to further convince current and future victims of cyber crimes to give out even more sensitive information like banking details. Another problem with this, is that attackers can log into individuals accounts themselves and get the information they need even easier.
One solution to protect yourself from a data breach is installing auto protection software such as Bitdefender, what that does is that it offers real time malware scanning and anti phishing protection. Another way of preventing a data breach is having a multi factor authentication on your device, which allows you to have a 3 to 5 step verification process before logging into your account.
